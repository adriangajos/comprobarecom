    What's inside

    Everything a takeoff needs. Nothing to study first.

    Plain buttons, clear results, and the features estimators kept asking for. Including the new Marking tools further down.

    Measure

    Areas, lengths and counts

    Trace an outline for square metres, click along a run for metres, tap items to count them. Every result lands in the panel with its own colour, and the totals update as you go.

    NewMarking

    Colour the drawing in before anything gets built.

    Marking lets you fill parts of a drawing with colour, add text and place images on it. See how the hull looks in red instead of blue, show a client which rooms get new flooring, or print the plan with your own logo in the corner.

    As imported With Marking
    Drag me

    • Fill any shape

      Pick a colour and an opacity, then click inside an outline. Change your mind as often as you like, every fill stays editable.

    • See the change before you make it

      Try a different colour for the hull, the roof or the facade and look at it next to the original. Cheaper than finding out after the paint is ordered.

    • Add your own branding

      Place a PNG or a PDF on the drawing, like your company logo, a stamp or a product image. Every print goes out looking like yours.

    • Text where you need it

      Room names, notes, colour codes. Write straight on the drawing instead of in a separate email.

    • Print it or export a PDF

      Markings sit on their own layer. Hide them with one click to get the clean drawing back, or send the coloured version to the client.

    How it works

    From a PDF to a number you can quote.

    The same five steps whether it's a two-room flat or a forty-metre hull.

    01

    Open the drawing

    Import a PDF or open a DXF. Scanned plans go through PDF to DXF first, so you get real lines to snap to instead of pixels.

    PDF · DXF · scanned PDF · CPB
    02

    Set the scale once

    Calibrate against one dimension you know, or let Detect Scale read it off the drawing. After that every number is in real units.

    Calibrate · Detect Scale
    03

    Click around the parts you need

    Trace areas, lay down lengths, count items. Each one gets a colour and a label right on the drawing.

    Area · Multiple Areas · Length · Count
    04

    Group it, save it, reuse it

    Results stack up on the right in groups with subtotals. Save the set to Memory and keep the whole job in one .cpb file.

    Groups · Memory · .cpb
    05

    Mark it up and send it

    Colour the drawing in, add your logo and a few notes, then print it or export a PDF for the client.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask before buying.

    Something missing? Write to us, a real person answers.

    What counts as a station?

    One license activates Comprobare on one Windows computer. If you need it on two machines you need two licenses, or email us for a team quote.

    Can I move my license to a new computer?

    Yes. Email us from the address you bought the license with and we'll reset the activation, so the same key works on the new machine.

    What happens when the annual license runs out?

    The app asks for a valid license again. Nothing is deleted: your .cpb project files stay on your disk, and a renewed key picks up right where you left off. The lifetime license never runs out.

    How do I get the license key?

    You pay through PayU by card or bank transfer. As soon as the payment is confirmed we email you the license key and a VAT invoice.

    Do I get an invoice?

    Yes. Every purchase comes with a full VAT invoice made out to the billing details you enter on the order form.

    Is Marking included in my license?

    Yes. There are no add-ons or paid tiers. Marking is part of the app for everyone with a license, and it's in the free trial too.

    Can I get a refund?

    The 7-day trial is the full application, so you can test everything before paying. Because of that, activated licenses can't be refunded, except where the law requires it or when a technical problem we can't fix stops you using the app. The details are in the purchase terms.
