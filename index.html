How it works

From file to figure in under five minutes.

A four-step flow that's the same whether you're measuring a 2-bedroom apartment or a 90-metre ship hull.

01STEP

Open or convert your drawing

Drop in a DXF or PDF. For paper plans, "Convert PDF" turns a scan into a clean, measurable drawing — pick low / med / high to match scan quality.

DXF · PDF · scanned PDF · CPB
02STEP

Set the scale once

Calibrate from a known dimension on the drawing, or let Detect Scale set it automatically. Comprobare locks the scale into the project — every measurement after that is in real units.

Calibrate · Detect Scale
03STEP

Click around the parts you care about

Trace areas with a polygon, lay down lengths point to point, mark off zones to subtract. Each measurement appears in the right-hand panel with its area, length, factor and total — colour-coded.

Areas · lengths · factors · subtractions
04STEP

Save, reuse, export

Save the whole project as .cpb. Save snapshots of measurement sets to the memory panel — reopen them next time, or load them onto a similar drawing in seconds.

.cpb · snapshots · copy · export PDF

FAQ

Before you buy — the usual questions.

What counts as a station?+

One license activates Comprobare on one Windows computer (workstation). If you need it on two machines, you need two licenses — or email us for a team quote.

Can I move my license to a new computer?+

Yes. Email us from your license email and we'll reset the activation so you can activate the same key on the new machine.

What happens when the annual license expires?+

The app asks for a valid license again — nothing is deleted. Your .cpb project files stay on your disk, and a renewed key picks up right where you left off. The lifetime license never expires.

How do I get the license key?+

You pay securely via PayU (card or bank transfer). Your license key and a VAT invoice are emailed to you right after the payment is confirmed.

Do I get an invoice?+

Yes — every purchase comes with a full VAT invoice issued to the billing details you enter on the order form.

Can I get a refund?+

The 7-day trial is the full application, so you can test everything before paying. Because of that, activated licenses are non-refundable except where required by law or when a technical issue we can't resolve prevents use — details in the purchase terms.
